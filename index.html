Field Manual

Controls

  • WS Drive forward / reverse
  • AD Turn hull
  • Mouse Aim turret (independent of hull)
  • Left click Fire
  • R Reload a partially-empty clip
  • Space Brake
  • Tab Switch allied tank
  • Wheel Zoom · Esc Pause

Tactics

  • Armor is directional. Front plates are thickest; side and rear hits do far more damage (rear hits get a bonus, shown with “!”).
  • Rocks and ruins block movement and direct-fire shells. Forests slow you down but grant 20% damage reduction.
  • Artillery lobs shells over obstacles onto the landing ring. It cannot fire inside its minimum range.
  • Regen spots marked with a green cross heal 15 HP per second. Fall back to them when damaged!
  • Uncontrolled allies fight on their own. If your tank dies you take over the next ally.
